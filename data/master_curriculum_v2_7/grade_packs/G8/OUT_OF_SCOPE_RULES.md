# G8 out-of-scope guardrails
- N-8-4: do not use two non-adjacent terms (neither is the first term) to reverse-solve first term/common difference as a standard task.
- N-8-5: do not give the sum and ask students to reverse-solve first term, number of terms or common difference as a standard task.
- N-8-6: do not use two non-adjacent geometric-sequence terms (neither is the first term) to reverse-solve first term/common ratio as a standard task.
- S-8-2: do NOT teach the polygon exterior-angle-sum formula as standard G8 content.
- A-8-3: do not use synthetic division / separated-coefficient division; polynomial product degree is at most 3 and dividend for division is quadratic in the official scope.
- A-8-5: factorization is restricted to integer-coefficient quadratic forms and direct multiplication-formula cases; do not introduce general two-variable homogeneous/nonhomogeneous factorization.
- F-8-1: do NOT introduce the abstract f(x) notation as the standard way to define functions; use correspondence and y=ax+b / y=c.
- Quadratic equations: no complex roots. Interpret 'no real solution' only within real-number context.
- Geometry: similarity, circle geometry, centers of triangles and trigonometric ratios belong to G9.
