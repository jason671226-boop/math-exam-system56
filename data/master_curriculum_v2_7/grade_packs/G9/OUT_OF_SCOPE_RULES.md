# G9 out-of-scope guardrails
- S-9-4: without a calculator, standard special angles are 30°, 45°, 60°. Do not introduce the full high-school trigonometric-function framework.
- S-9-5~S-9-7: do not add deleted/extended circle topics such as tangent-chord angle, two-circle relations, or common tangents as standard G9 content.
- S-9-11: proofs must be direct consequences of released learning content; do not require extended theorems beyond the curriculum scope.
- S-9-13: in the official scope, volume is required for right prisms; surface area is addressed for right prisms, right circular cones and regular pyramids. Do not introduce cone/pyramid volume formulas as standard G9 requirements.
- F-9-2: focus on graphical properties of y=ax², y=ax²+k, y=a(x-h)², y=a(x-h)²+k. General completion-of-square transformation and general quadratic-function application are G10.
- F-9-2: use only already-completed-square forms for standard maximum/minimum questions.
- D-9-2: probability tree diagrams are limited to two levels, mainly symmetric branching.
- D-9-3: classical probability uses symmetric situations; non-symmetric objects are for experimental exploration, not 'equally likely' counting.
- Do not introduce high-school permutations/combinations as the standard method for G9 probability.
