# G4 out-of-scope guardrails
- Fractions: same-denominator arithmetic and equivalent fractions are in scope; unlike-denominator arithmetic belongs to G5.
- Decimals: two decimal places and decimal addition/subtraction are in scope; do not move to general decimal×decimal or decimal÷decimal.
- Geometry: formal degree measure, perpendicular/parallel, congruence, triangle/quadrilateral classification are in scope. Triangle/quadrilateral interior-angle-sum formalization belongs to later work.
- Area: formal rectangle/square area and square meter are in scope; triangle/parallelogram/trapezoid area formulas belong to G5.
- Volume: cubic centimeter and unit-cube counting are in scope; formal rectangular-prism volume formula and cubic meter belong later.
- Time: compound-unit addition/subtraction is in scope; time multiplication/division belongs to G5.
- Division: two-digit divisors are in scope; keep within integer division and grade-appropriate estimation.
- Statistics: bar and line graphs are in scope; pie charts belong to G6.
