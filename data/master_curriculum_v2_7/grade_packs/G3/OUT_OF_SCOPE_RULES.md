# G3 out-of-scope guardrails
- Fractions: keep to simple fractions and same-denominator comparison/addition/subtraction. Do not generate unlike-denominator arithmetic.
- Decimals: keep to one decimal place. Do not generate two-decimal-place curriculum tasks as standard G3 content.
- Length: millimeter is in scope; kilometer is later.
- Area: square centimeter and unit-square counting are in scope. Do not prematurely teach formal rectangle/square area formulas as the core G3 rule; these become formalized later.
- Volume: not a G3 standard topic.
- Time: 24-hour system and elapsed time are in scope, but avoid calendar-duration combinatorics beyond grade expectations.
- Geometry: angle types, rectangle/square properties and circles are in scope; formal angle-degree measurement is later.
- Division: divisor is one digit in the official G3 scope.
