# G7 out-of-scope guardrails
- Absolute value: use for number-line distance and magnitude. Do NOT generate absolute-value equations or absolute-value inequalities as standard G7 tasks.
- Scientific notation: focus on representation, conversion and magnitude. Do NOT make scientific-notation four-operation calculation a standard requirement.
- Exponents: N-7-6/N-7-7 use nonnegative integer exponents for the standard laws. Negative exponent appears only in explaining scientific notation of small positive numbers, not as a general exponent-law topic.
- Ratio/proportion: do not use complex fractions as the standard method.
- One-variable inequalities: solve a single linear inequality. Do NOT require solving compound/double inequalities such as 3 ≤ 2x-1 ≤ 5.
- Linear systems: geometric interpretation is limited to two lines intersecting in exactly one point as the standard G7 case.
- Three views: solids are limited to non-hollow arrangements embeddable in a 3×3×3 cube.
- Geometry: do not introduce G8 angle relations, triangle congruence proofs, Pythagorean theorem or formal proof sequences as standard G7 content.
- Statistics: probability theory is not G7 core; G7 focuses statistical tables/graphs and mean/median/mode.
